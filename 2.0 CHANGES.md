# 2.0 Changes

- Removal of **Ad Astra**. Space progression will be overhauled in an upcoming update, but for now, the rocket tiers are preserved with tiered Space Probe launching.
- Changed worldgen to **No Man's Land**.
- Removal of **Tech Reborn**.
- **Create** has been updated to 0.6 and is also completely optional!
- MI Addons, including **Extended Industrialization**, **MI Tweaks**, **YAI!**, and **Industrialization Overdrive** flesh out the MI experience.
- **Better batching machines!** 2.0 has many more batching multiblocks!
- **New processing lines!** New ores and materials open up more possibilities for automation! Keep an eye out for Boron!
- **Better QoL!** With all these new mods and MI updates since 1.19.2, the QoL in the pack is night and day!
- **Focused Magic!** 1.0 had a number of magic mods that went unused. Those have been removed. The magic progression revolves around **Spectrum**.
- **Mod integration** is more robust, with many more mods having assembler recipes and such.
- **Companion Mod!** StaTech Industry 2.0 has a companion mod, increasing the ways we can customize the pack!
- **New surprises!** There are a number of differences from the original version. Better get out there and find them!
