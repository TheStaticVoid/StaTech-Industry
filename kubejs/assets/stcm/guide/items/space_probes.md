# Space Probes

<ItemImage id="kubejs:basic_space_probe" />
<ItemImage id="kubejs:advanced_space_probe" />
<ItemImage id="kubejs:highly_advanced_space_probe" />
<ItemImage id="kubejs:space_probe" />

The various Space Probes are mining devices that can be launched into space using the Space Probe Launcher. They can retrieve a multitude of advanced materials that allow you to do various things, such as create multiblocks, advanced materials used in nuclear reactors, etc.
