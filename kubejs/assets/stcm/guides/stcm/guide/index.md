# StaTech Industry

Welcome to **StaTech Industry 2**!

This new major version of the modpack includes many new nachines and batching multiblock variants of machines. This guide will contain most information related to specific additions, features and configurations of StaTech Industry and will act as a replacement to Ponder, which was previously used to show multiblock structures.
