# Utilities

<ItemImage id="kubejs:angel_ring" />

The Angel Ring allows you to gain infinite, free creative flight when worn. It can be made as early as getting a Chemical Reactor (to access the End) if you complete Spectrum! It can also be acquired later on with pure technological advances.

<ItemImage id="kubejs:fireproof_ring" />

The Fireproof Ring is (currently) a magic-only acquirable source of infinite fire resistance that frees you of worrying about lava or fire damage when worn.
