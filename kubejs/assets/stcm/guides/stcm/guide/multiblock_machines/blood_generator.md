# Blood Generator

<GameScene zoom="1" interactive={true} fullWidth={true}>
    <MultiblockShape controller="modern_industrialization:blood_generator" />
</GameScene>

The **Blood Generator** multiblock can output up to **8192 EU/t**, the **maximum cable transfer speed** of the **HV tier**. Blood is consumed at **1024 EU/mB** within the multiblock. **Nutrient Rich Dragon's Breath** is also able to be used and is consumed at **8192 EU/mB**.
