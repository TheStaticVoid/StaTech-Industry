# Processors

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="logic_processor" scale="4" />

  <ItemImage id="calculation_processor" scale="4" />

  <ItemImage id="engineering_processor" scale="4" />
</Row>

Processors are one of the primary ingredients in AE2 [devices](../ae2-mechanics/devices.md) and machines. They are also one of your first
big automation challenges. There are three types of processor, made with gold, <ItemLink id="certus_quartz_crystal" />,
and diamond respectively. They are made using [presses](presses.md) in an Assembler, in a multi-step
process (usually achieved via a series of Packers, Assemblers and filtered piping).

## Production Steps

<Column gap="5">
  1.  Gather/make the required ingredients: silicon, molten redstone, gold, <ItemLink id="certus_quartz_crystal" />, diamond.

  <br />

  2.  Press the prerequisite printed circuit components in a Packer

  <br />

  3.  Finally, assemble them with Molten Redstone in an Assembler

</Column>
