Because of how MI handles datagen you cannot rely on it to create recolored coils with CTM.

You may notice that the coil_top.png texture is a sprite sheet instead of a single texture. Do not actually use this for the top coil texture. instead you will want to cut this image up into 4 pieces and create a new folder in the continuity directory. Replace coil_top.png with coil_side.png when you are done.

see ZedTech-MI/assets/continuity/optifine/ctm/Zedtech MI/coils/ for an example of how this works.
